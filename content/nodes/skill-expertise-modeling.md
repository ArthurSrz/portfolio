# Expertise Modeling

Eliciting tacit expert reasoning and encoding it into structured, versionable representations that AI systems can execute faithfully.

## Core Competencies

- **Expertise Elicitation**: Capturing domain expert knowledge through structured interviews and observation
- **Reasoning Graph Design**: Translating expert decision logic into graph-based reasoning packages
- **Knowledge Versioning**: Managing the evolution of encoded expertise as domains change
- **Human-AI Collaboration**: Designing systems where expert judgment and AI execution complement each other

## Theoretical Grounding

Rooted in Marvin Minsky's Society of Mind — modeling expertise not as a monolithic block but as a collection of specialized reasoning agents that collaborate to produce expert-level judgment.

## Distinction from Knowledge Graph Design

While [Knowledge Graph Design](/nodes/skill-knowledge-graphs) focuses on structural data modeling (schemas, ontologies, graph databases), Expertise Modeling addresses the upstream challenge: how to capture the reasoning processes that populate those structures.
