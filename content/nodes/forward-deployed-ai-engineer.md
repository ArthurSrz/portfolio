As a Forward Deployed AI Engineer at Clarifeye, I capture and encode expert business logic into governed AI teammates. Drawing on Minsky's Society of Mind intuition, I model expertise not as a monolithic system but as a collection of specialized reasoning agents that collaborate to produce expert-level judgment.

## Reasoning Graph Packages

Design structured representations of expert decision-making that AI systems can execute faithfully while remaining fully interpretable. This involves:

- **Expertise Elicitation**: Structured sessions with domain specialists to capture tacit knowledge
- **Graph-Based Encoding**: Translating expert reasoning into Reasoning Graph Packages using Clara
- **Knowledge Versioning**: Managing the evolution of encoded expertise as domains change

## Governed AI Teammates

Build AI agents that operate within explicit governance boundaries defined by expert-encoded reasoning:

- **Interpretable Execution**: Every AI decision traceable back to expert-defined reasoning paths
- **Human-AI Collaboration**: Expert judgment and AI execution complement each other
- **Domain Fidelity**: AI teammates apply the same reasoning processes human experts would

**Stack**: Python, Neo4j, Clara (Clarifeye's proprietary reasoning engine)
